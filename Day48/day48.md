# Day 48 – The Verdict Engine

## Project

The Verdict Engine is a laptop comparison and decision-support
application for students choosing a laptop on a budget.

## Features
- Adjustable criteria weights
- Live ranking updates
- Overall scoring
- Source citations
- Research methodology panel
- Conflict resolution explanation
