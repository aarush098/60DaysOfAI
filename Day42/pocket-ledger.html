Habit checklist

5 tips for allowance control

  1. Split the day you get paid: move a fixed % to "goals" immediately, before it hits your wallet.
  2. Log spends same-day — memory of small purchases fades within 24 hours.
  3. Give subscriptions a monthly review; cancel anything unused for 30+ days.
  4. Use the 24-hour rule for non-essential purchases over ₹300.
  5. Round up spare change from cash purchases into your goal jar.

Prompts to ask Claude

"I overspent on food this week — help me plan next week's meals within ₹500."
"Explain compound interest like I'm saving ₹200/week for a year."
"Review my subscription list and tell me which ones aren't worth it."
